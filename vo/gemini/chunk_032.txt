Ned dies in the workhouse infirmary, owing money to three taverns. A parish clerk sends a letter addressed to "the widow." You read it standing at the sink. You're fifty years old, and for the first time since you were nineteen, you are legally nobody's wife. You don't cry. For a while you don't feel anything at all. Then you sit down on the kitchen floor and feel everything at once, for about an hour, until Sam finds you and sits down on the floor next to you without saying a word.
