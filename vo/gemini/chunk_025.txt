Ned unties the straw from your neck and lets it drop onto the cobbles. Then he shakes your new owner's hand, and the three of you go to the pub and sit at one table, because that is also the custom. Everyone laughs. You are the only one who doesn't. Your daughter doesn't wave. She's twelve. She has already decided something about you, and it will take years to find out what. The truth is, none of it counts. The sale has no legal force, and a court, if anyone asked, would tell you so. In the years ahead, courts will increasingly do just that. You've been sold, and you're still married. Both things are true. You're the only person in the Bull Ring who finds that strange.
