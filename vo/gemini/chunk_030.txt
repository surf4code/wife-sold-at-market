You're forty when Ned turns up at your door. The barmaid has left him, taking the baby and most of the furniture. He's thinner. He's sober enough to be polite. He hasn't come to take you back. He's come for your wages. Turns out, he's been to see a clerk. He's been told, correctly, what you've never been able to forget. The sale meant nothing. You're still his wife. Everything you earn is his. He doesn't need to prove that he sold you. He only needs to prove that he married you. Immediately, you want to fight. Sam stays quiet. Then he points out, gently, that in a court somebody will ask what exactly happened at the Bull Ring that Thursday, and nobody in the room will be able to answer it safely.
